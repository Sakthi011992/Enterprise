Shell rules

The top bar is the same on every screen: search and jump (Ctrl K), outlet, language, dark mode, notifications and account. Items without permission are hidden. Items missing from the plan show a lock to Admins only. Staff can pin up to 5 items. The menu collapses to an icon rail below 1280 px and becomes a drawer below 768 px.
